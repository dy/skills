# Questions by stage

Source corpus: [dy/contemplation](https://github.com/dy/contemplation) — "Questions That See".
Tags: **[evidence]** — answer from the artifact with citations or UNKNOWN; **[ask]** — sharpen
and return to the author, never fake-answer; **[hybrid]** — attempt from evidence, mark the
part only the author can answer. Every stage ends in its **artifact** — a decision, a list,
a verdict; a stage without its artifact is unfinished.

---

## ∞ INTENTION — offering or ego trip? `[ask]`

> *"Whatever you do — do that as an offering to Me."* — Krishna (BG 9.27)
> *"Art in myself, not myself in art."* — Stanislavski

Wholly the author's. The agent contributes only **ego-signal observations** from the
artifact (boasting copy vs delivered reality, vanity metrics, cleverness that performs,
features built for the demo not the user) — stated as artifact-vs-claim tensions, never as
verdicts on motive.

- What's the real motive — service or show? Would I do this if no one ever knew?
- What am I proving? What insecurity is this compensating for?
- What would remain if applause disappeared? If all metrics disappeared?
- Is this a gift or a transaction? Would I be proud to show this to my teacher?
- If this succeeds perfectly, what's different in reality? Can it not be done? `[hybrid — impact side is evidence]`
- What paradox lives here — what opposite is secretly true?

**Artifact:** one honest sentence: why this exists.

## 1. EXPLORE — what problem wants solving? `[evidence]`

> *"Make something people want."* — Y Combinator
> *"It is by logic that we prove, but by intuition that we discover."* — Poincaré

**The problem**
- Show me one person's exact frustration — the precise moment it hits. What emotion at the friction point?
- What's the villain in the user's story? What would they complain about to a friend?
- What friction does this remove? What becomes possible that wasn't before?
- What would the world lose if this never existed?
- Is this genuinely serving the person — or just giving them what they want? `[hybrid]`

**The people**
- Who wants this desperately? Who is NOT the user? Who must be disappointed?
- Who would abuse or misuse it? Who depends on it? Who celebrates when it arrives?
- What audience would love this but doesn't know it exists yet?

**The territory**
- What already exists? Best and worst of it? What's everyone doing that must be avoided?
- What obvious solution is everyone ignoring? Who already solved an adjacent pain — what did they get right/miss?
- What constraints, dependencies, limitations bind this? What category does it create — or belong to?
- All the ways this could be useful: main, hidden, indirect, unintended? What does it replace?
- What's the simplest thing that could possibly work?

**The lenses** *(fan out as independent subagents on deep runs)*
- **Practitioner** — works with this daily: what do they know that academics miss?
- **Academic** — what does the evidence actually say? Where does it contradict popular belief?
- **Skeptic** — strongest counterargument? What do proponents conveniently ignore?
- **Economist** — who profits from the current narrative? What incentives shape it?
- **Historian** — what parallel played out before, and how did it end?

**The window**
- Why now — what changed, what's closing? Cost of delay vs gift of waiting?
- What trend is this riding or fighting?

**Artifact:** the problem in one sentence + the one person it's for + why now.

## 2. PROJECT — one job, what must it do perfectly? `[evidence-attempt]`

> *"Perfection is achieved not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away."* — Saint-Exupéry
> *"The ideal machine is no machine — the function is performed without any mechanism."* — Altshuller

Attempt every question from the artifact; where the artifact is silent, UNKNOWN — and an
UNKNOWN essence is always finding № 1.

**Essence**
- If it could only do ONE thing, what survives the fire? What job is it hired for? What does it kill?
- Krishna identifies as the functional essence — taste in water, light in sun. What is this product's taste, its light?
- What would make it feel inevitable, not optional — canonical, referenced for years?
- One metric — what number defines victory? How does an early signal differ from noise?

**Pure form and minimum**
- Before compromise — what's the Platonic ideal? The limit as versions → ∞?
- What's the theoretical minimum — one function, one screen, one sentence: what survives?
- What's the seed that contains the whole tree?
- What contradiction must this hold without resolving?

**Single-player value**
- What does one user get, alone, before anyone else joins? What makes the first user stay?

**Boundary**
- What's out? What must it NOT become — what would betray it?
- What feature will users beg for that must be lovingly refused?
- What's bone vs flesh? What strength becomes weakness if taken too far?

**Soul**
- What's the moat — what can't be copied? What makes it alive, not just useful? `[hybrid]`
- Where is the beauty hiding? What's the punchline — what makes a stranger say "whoa"?
- What's the thing only we can see? `[ask]`

**Spine**
- Happy path — what do 90% of users actually do? What pulls everything into orbit?
- What breaks first under weight/scale? Which edge cases matter, which are noise?

**Price** `[ask]`
- Every yes is a no — what's being traded? What sacrifice will be regretted? What kept that should be sacrificed?

**The unlock**
- What decision, if made, makes all others obvious? What minimal angle shift unlocks a whole new value layer?
- What is the author avoiding committing to? `[ask]` (Reversible → move fast. Irreversible → sleep on it.)

**Name**
- Does the name reveal or betray? Would it embarrass in 5 years?
- Is this so clear a stranger would grasp it? (Hilbert: complete only when explainable to the first person on the street.)

**Artifact:** essence in one sentence + one metric + the boundary (what it must NOT become).

## 3. EXECUTE — ship incomplete but useful `[evidence]`

> *"You have a right to perform your prescribed duty, but you are not entitled to the fruits of action."* — Krishna (BG 2.47)
> *"Do things that don't scale."* — Paul Graham
> *"Premature optimization is the root of all evil."* — Knuth

**Riskiest assumption**
- What must be true for this to work? What lie about feasibility is hiding in plain sight?
- Smallest proof of value? Cheapest way to learn we're wrong? What can be faked before built?
- What's fool's gold here? What premature optimization is stealing time from shipping?
- Can one person pre-pay or pre-commit before the code exists?

**Next move**
- Smallest shippable thing that's genuinely useful — the first term of the series that already captures the dominant behavior?
- What's the 20% that delivers 80%? What would embarrass to NOT ship?

**Deadline** `[ask]`
- No deadline = no ship. What's the date? One day left — what survives? One hour — what's the one thing?

**Energy** `[ask]`
- What excites — chase it. What drains — eliminate it. Where's momentum? What feels like play?
- What is being done only because of "should"? What would be done even if it failed?
- What am I afraid to ship? Ship that.

**Learning**
- Learning or just building? What was learned this week that changed the plan? `[evidence: commits/issues/changelog]`
- What signal from real users surprised? Where is the model of the user provably wrong?
- What habit feels productive but produces no learning?

**Artifact:** the riskiest assumption + the cheapest test of it + one shippable next move.

## 4. REVISE — ready to offer? `[evidence]`

> *"Have the confidence to cut."* — Paul Graham
> *"Good design is invisible."* — Don Norman
> *Ready when: adding more would make it worse; removing anything would make it incomplete.*

Priority: **Truth > Users > Developers > Agents > Ecosystem** (if a dedicated readiness
skill is installed, delegate the pillar audit to it and fold its verdict in).

**Truth** — why does this exist?
- What exact pain does this heal — one sentence? Is it honest — claims only what it delivers?
- Is it complete — nothing half-done, nothing excess? Will it last — stable foundations, minimal dependencies?
- Is it elegant — simple core, complex only where necessary?
- Don't delete out of ascetic pride, don't keep out of attachment — keep only what serves (yukta-vairagya).

**Users** — can they succeed without help?
- Start in 5 minutes: install, run, see it work? Does it do what it promises?
- Do errors help recovery? Are defaults sensible? Follow the README literally — does it work?

**Developers** — can they contribute without asking?
- Can they find things? Do names reveal intent? Do tests exist, pass, give confidence?
- Is everything in the repo — no hidden knowledge? Could a stranger maintain this if the author disappeared?

**Agents** — can AI work here autonomously?
- Are instructions sufficient? Is feedback fast (lint/test under 2 minutes)? Are patterns consistent?
- Could an agent complete a task here without asking questions?

**Ecosystem** — does it play well with others?
- Conventions followed? Integrates cleanly? Runtime requirements documented? Metadata complete?
- Would an expert in this ecosystem approve?

**Claims vs reality** — does every published claim resolve to something real *right now*?
- License claimed (manifest/README) vs LICENSE file actually present?
- Repo/homepage URLs vs actual current location (follow the redirects)?
- Version tags vs registry releases — is what users install represented in git?
- Badges vs live CI — does the pipeline exist and run against current code?
- README examples vs actual API — run them literally; stated platform/runtime support vs anything that tests it?

**The cut**
- What can be deleted entirely? Start there. If half must go — which half lives?
- What's one removal that improves everything? What cognitive load can be lifted?
- What's already complete that's being added to unnecessarily?

**The craftsman's eye**
- What's ugly, clunky, slow, wasteful — embarrassing to look at?
- What's the one fix that improves many things at once?
- What's coupled that shouldn't be? Symptom vs cause? What breaks if requirements change?

**Time**
- What would embarrass in 6 months? Timeless or trendy? What principle guides all future changes?
- What docs would future maintainers/agents/users need?

**Pressure**
- What constraint, if imposed, would force brilliance — half the time, half the features: which half lives?
- 10x users tomorrow — what shatters first? What would a ruthless editor cut without mercy?
- What would remain if every line had to be defended?

**Honest retro** `[hybrid]`
- No ego — what actually happened? What worked, what failed, what never again?
- What honest insight is being sat on? What would be confessed under full honesty? `[ask]`

**Artifact:** verdict (Ready / Nearly / Needs work / Early) + kill list + the one fix that improves many things.

## ∞ BLINDSPOTS — what am I not seeing? `[evidence — from the OUTSIDE stance]`

> *"To find new ideas you have to seize on signs of breakage instead of looking away."* — Paul Graham
> *"A good new idea has to seem bad to most people, or someone would have already explored it."* — Paul Graham

The one stage the author structurally cannot run alone. Answer from non-investment;
hypotheses about the author's psychology are marked as hypotheses and returned as questions.

**Forces**
- What strength is underused? What weakness is not being admitted?
- What's changing in the world that makes this more or less valuable right now? What timing advantage won't last?
- Who approaches this from a different angle — what threatens the whole premise?

**The ignored**
- What assumption breaks under pressure — the theorem that fails at the boundary?
- What disaster is being treated as if it won't happen?
- What's being made harder than it needs to be? What expectation is violated — what's confusing or surprising here?

**The comfortable lie** `[hybrid — hypothesize from evidence, return as question]`
- What pattern repeats unconsciously? What story is the author attached to that holds this back?
- What failure mode is being romanticized? What success secretly feared?
- What if the obstacle IS the way? What opposite of the core belief is equally true?

**The dodged question**
- What's the hardest question here — the one that keeps returning no matter how often dismissed?
- Is this even the right problem? What's the question behind the question?
- What UNKNOWNs from earlier stages cluster around one avoided topic? ← the dodge, made visible.

**The enemy's 5 seconds** *(run as an independent lens)*
- What would the harshest critic notice immediately? What would a competitor exploit?
- What's too obvious for the author to notice — the water they swim in?
- What's most valuable may look most ordinary; what looks impressive may be empty. Which is which here?

**Artifact:** the top 3 blindspots with evidence + the dodged question, named.

## ∞ WONDER — where's the hidden gold? `[evidence-attempt]`

> *"When an idea seems simultaneously novel and obvious, it's probably a good one."* — Paul Graham
> *"One of the most powerful kinds of copying is to copy something from one field into another."* — Paul Graham

**Unintended value**
- What value was never intended — what adjacent problem is accidentally solved?
- What constraint, removed — or introduced — changes everything?
- What blessing is disguised as obstacle? What's the low-hanging fruit no one picked?

**Reversal**
- What if this is about something else entirely? What reverses the assumptions?
- What would make this legendary, not just useful? What's the "aha" waiting to happen?

**Outsider eyes**
- What would a child see? An artist? A grandmother understand immediately? An alien find strange?
- What's obvious to a beginner that the expert misses?

**Magic**
- What would make this feel like magic, not mechanics? The easter egg, the wink, the secret gift for the discoverer?
- What would make someone screenshot it for a friend?

**Cross-domain**
- What field has the answer this is missing? What would this look like in music, gardening, cooking?
- What's the mashup no one's tried?

**Absurd but valid**
- The weirdest way this could help someone? What if 10x'd? 1/10th'd? What breaks if it succeeds wildly?

**Gut** `[ask]`
- What does gut know that analysis doesn't? What's the answer already had but not admitted?
- What's this secretly about — the thing no one's saying?

**Artifact:** 2–3 hidden-gold items, each with its smallest unlock.

## ∞ META — choosing what to work on `[ask, evidence for history]`

> *"If you asked an oracle the secret to doing great work and the oracle replied with a single word, my bet would be on 'curiosity.'"* — Paul Graham

- What question, answered honestly, renders all current activity either obviously right or obviously wasted?
- Am I contemplating because I'm seeking truth — or because building is scary?
- What is compounding — what work done today makes tomorrow's easier? Deep or spreading thin? `[hybrid: repo/commit history is evidence]`
- What's the learning velocity — how fast does signal arrive from real humans? Feedback loop or echo chamber? `[hybrid]`
- Is this the right problem — or just the nearest one? What's unfashionable but important?
- What looks boring to others but fascinates me? That's the signal.
- What would be built in one weekend on pure intuition, no plan?

**Artifact:** keep / double-down / stop — one verb per current thread.

## ∞ FEEDBACK — distribution `[evidence]`

> *"The best product is the one that distributes itself."*
> *"Network effects account for 70% of all value created in technology since 1994."* — NFX

**Value proposition (SPIN)**
- Situation — what are they doing today without this?
- Problem — where exactly does it hurt?
- Implication — what gets worse unsolved? What does inaction cost daily?
- Payoff — what becomes possible once solved? Say it in THEIR words, not the author's.

**Spread**
- What makes a user tell one friend — the "you gotta see this" moment?
- Is there a multiplayer moment? What's the viral loop: user does X → friend sees Y → friend joins?

**Where the people already are**
- What do they already read, watch, subscribe to, attend? Which community is one genuine conversation away?
- What adjacent product's users are underserved and would love this?
- What's the "Collison installation" — can they be set up on the spot?

**Cold start**
- What's single-player value before the network exists? What's the contained fire — the deliberately narrow market to ignite first?
- Which side of the marketplace is hard — supply or demand? Can one side be seeded?

**Compounding**
- What gets better the more it's used — data, content, network, trust?
- Retention > acquisition: do users come back? How often? Why?
- What asset can competitors not replicate by launch day?
- Does the quality of the offering create its own distribution field — is this inevitable?

**Reality check**
- Building audience, or just building product? What went to real users this week; what signal came back? `[hybrid]`
- Can it be pre-sold? What's the cheapest way to put this in front of 10 strangers today?

**Artifact:** the SPIN in the user's words + first-10-users plan + the one loop worth building.
