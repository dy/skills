# The Marketing Canon — who to trust, and why

Ranked by **verified track record × actionability** after an adversarial audit that discounted self-reported claims and the *guru loop* (using a teaching business as proof of what's being taught). Operator before teacher; acquisition > revenue claim > testimonial. Use this to decide whose advice to weight — and whose mythology to ignore.

## The core stack (what this skill is built on)

A pipeline: diagnose → headline/test → prove → offer → position → brand → spread → persuade.

## High-value, more specialized

- **Gary Bencivenga** — *Marketing Bullets* (free) — the Persuasion Equation; "the inverse of the guru" (corpus is free, income was client fees).
- **Dan Kennedy** — *The Ultimate Sales Letter*, *No B.S. Direct Marketing* — the 3Ms (Message-Market-Media); validated by Brunson *buying* his IP.
- **Russell Brunson** — *DotCom/Expert/Traffic Secrets* — Value Ladder, Hook-Story-Offer, Dream 100 (largely repackaged Kennedy/Abraham — credit the origins).
- **Gary Halbert** — *The Boron Letters* (free archive) — Starving Crowd, A-pile/B-pile (built it before teaching it; the Coat-of-Arms letter is independently corroborated).
- **Robert Collier** — *The Robert Collier Letter Book* (1931) — "enter the conversation already in the prospect's mind."
- **Joe Sugarman** — *The Adweek Copywriting Handbook* — the Slippery Slide + psychological triggers. Learn the craft, **not** the ethics (documented 1989 FTC consent order for faked editorial independence).

## Tools, not authorities (use the framework, discount the guru)

- **Donald Miller / StoryBrand SB7** — a fine homepage wireframe; every "doubled revenue" case is self-sourced; output is formulaic. A floor, not a ceiling.
- **Seth Godin** — strategy and reframing, near-zero execution mechanics (lowest actionability in the set, by his own design). His one post-1998 operating venture (Squidoo) failed.
- **Ries & Trout / Positioning** — foundational *vocabulary*; the "22 Immutable Laws" were empirically refuted (Ehrenberg-Bass) and predicted the iPhone would fail. Treat as hypotheses, not laws.
- **Gary Vaynerchuk** — the attention/distribution meta-framework (*Day Trading Attention*) is usable; discount the operator mythology (VeeFriends 200k+ supply dump after a $90M raise; self-reported Wine Library revenue).
- **Renvoisé & Morin / Neuromarketing, The Persuasion Code** — the message-packaging checklist (six stimuli, four steps, building blocks) is a usable pre-send discipline that independently converges with Hopkins/PAS/Heath; discount the neuroscience wrapper (triune "reptilian brain" is discredited anatomy — their own 2018 "primal brain"/System-1 reframe concedes it) and note the books partly market their NeuroMap™ consultancy. Craft: yes. Brain-science authority: no.

## What to discount everywhere

Self-reported revenue, "X students earned $Y", lifetime-revenue figures that fluctuate across interviews ($9B → $21.7B → $75B), and testimonials that circulate only inside the seller's own ecosystem. **Trust** acquisitions with public filings, third-party trade press, peer-reviewed studies, and decades-long campaign longevity. Even strong operators inflate the *ceiling* ("you'll get this too") while the *floor* ("I did this") is real — weight the floor.
